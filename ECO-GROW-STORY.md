# The story the site isn't telling

Read off the ECO-GROW label in `public/media/shamba-46.webp` — Shamba Connect's
own product, photographed on Mercy's own balcony:

> **ECO-GROW ORGANIC FOLIAR**
> *Based on rabbit urine.* 100% organic.
> Promotes root formation · Increases beneficial microbes · Locks in soil
> nutrients · Fixes nitrogen in the soil.
> Mix 250ml in 20 litres of water. Spray on leaves and stems, twice a week for
> three weeks. Best for vegetative crops and fruit trees.

## Why this matters

Every other section of the site sells one thing at a time: rabbits here,
gardens there, seedlings in the shop. The label says they are the same thing.

    rabbits  →  urine  →  ECO-GROW foliar  →  the gardens they install
       ↑                                              ↓
       └──────────  meat, breeding stock  ←───────────┘

Shamba Connect keeps rabbits. The rabbits produce urine. The urine is bottled
as an organic foliar feed. The feed grows the kitchen gardens they design and
install. The gardens are why people buy the seedlings. Nothing leaves the loop.

**That is the one thing a competitor selling spices and rabbit meat cannot
copy.** They can sell rabbit. They can sell produce. They cannot sell a closed
system, because a closed system is not a product line — it is years of running
both halves at once.

It is also completely verifiable. It is printed on the product.

## What it should change

1. **A section on the homepage that draws the loop.** Not a claim — a diagram
   with the four photographs that already exist: the rabbits (`shamba-15`),
   the jerrycan (`shamba-46`), the vertical garden (`shamba-12` or `24`), the
   harvest (`shamba-48`, Mercy holding lettuce). Four images, one arrow each.

2. **ECO-GROW gets a real product page**, not a shop tile. Dosage, what it
   does, what it is made of, which crops. It is the only own-brand
   manufactured item in the catalogue and it currently has one line of text.

3. **It answers "why organic?" without saying "100% organic".** The site says
   organic four times without ever explaining what makes it so. The answer is
   the loop.
